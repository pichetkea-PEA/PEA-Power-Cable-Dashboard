PEA Cable Asset Health Index & Risk Theory

Standard Operating Procedure & Diagnostic Evaluation Manual v2.4

IEEE 400.2 / IEC 60270 Compliant

1. Executive Summary & Diagnostic Philosophy

The Cable Health Index (HI) algorithm implemented in this platform is a condition-based, multi-parametric scoring engine that quantifies the real-time degradation state of underground power cable circuits (XLPE/EPR insulation systems, accessories, and sheath earthing configurations).

The calculated Health Index ranges from 0% (Critical / Severe Defect) to 100% (Pristine / Good Condition).

2. Multi-Parametric Mathematical Formulation

Base HI = 100 - ∑ ( Wi × Si )

Where Wi is the normalized weight factor (∑ Wi = 1.0) and Si is the parameter severity penalty score (0 to 100).

4. Critical Penalty Override Rule (Safety Ceiling)

Final HI = Min ( Base HI, Critical Safety Cap )

To prevent minor healthy parameters from masking a fatal localized defect:

  • If PD ≥ 1000 pC or Discharge = Surface / Corona: Final HI is capped at 45% (Poor/Critical).
  • If Tan Delta IEEE Status is Action Required: Final HI is capped at 45%.
  • If Temperature ≥ 90°C or IR < 1 GΩ: Final HI is capped at 40%.

5. Health Index Classification Bands & Action Protocols

Health Index Condition Action Protocol
85% - 100% Good Routine periodic inspection cycle (every 3 to 5 years).
70% - 84% Moderate Annual condition monitoring; trend analysis of telemetry.
50% - 69% Caution Schedule diagnostic re-test within 6 months; pinpoint joint issues.
30% - 49% Poor Plan refurbishment or replacement within 30 to 60 days.
0% - 29% Critical Immediate de-energization or load shift; emergency repair.

6. 3×3 Failure Risk Matrix

Risk Level = Probability of Failure (PoF) × Consequence of Failure (CoF)
  • Probability of Failure (PoF): High (HI < 50%), Medium (50% ≤ HI < 75%), Low (HI ≥ 75%).
  • Consequence of Failure (CoF): High (115/230 kV, Critical Substation, or Asset Value > 5M THB), Medium (22/33 kV Trunk Feeder), Low (Radial/Spur Feeder).
